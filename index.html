Privacy Policy for FinancePro

Last updated: October 2026

FinancePro is built with a strong commitment to your privacy. We use an "offline-first" architecture, meaning that you have full control over your financial data. The data we collect depends on how you choose to use the application.

1. Local Data (Offline Mode - Default)

When you download and use FinancePro without creating a cloud account, we do not collect any personal or financial data.

  • All your financial inputs (incomes, expenses, budgets, debts, and investments) are stored locally and securely on your device's internal storage.
  • Your security PIN code or biometric authentication data never leaves your device.
  • We cannot access, read, or share your local financial records.

2. Cloud Data (When Creating an Account / PRO Mode)

If you choose to register for a Cloud Workspace to synchronize your data across devices, back up your records, or access premium features, we collect and store the following information using secure, industry-standard third-party services (Google Firebase):

  • Account Information: We collect your email address and an encrypted password to create and secure your account.
  • Financial Data: Your encrypted financial data (transactions, accounts, categories, and budgets) is synchronized to our secure cloud database, strictly used to provide cross-device synchronization and backup features.

3. Payment Information

FinancePro offers Premium subscriptions and lifetime licenses via trusted third-party platforms (such as Selar or Google Play Billing).

  • We do not collect or store your raw credit card numbers or Mobile Money PINs. All payment transactions are processed securely by certified third-party payment gateways.

4. Device and Diagnostic Data

To improve the app's performance and fix bugs, we may collect anonymous, non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as crash logs and operating system versions) exclusively to maintain app stability.

5. Contact Us
